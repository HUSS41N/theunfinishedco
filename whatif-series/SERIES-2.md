# What If — Series 2: the rethink

The same ten premises, rewritten for story and retention, produced in **v2 (flat vector)**.
v1 versions remain (now in the new typography).

## What was wrong with Series 1 (diagnosis)
1. **The premise card killed the hook.** Every video cut from its strongest image to a text card at about 1.5 s. Now the premise is overlaid on the hook image and the shot keeps moving.
2. **The middles were lists:** lie 1, lie 2, lie 3. Repetition isn't escalation. Now every beat raises the cost, answers a question or plants a new one, and something changes on screen every 2–4 s.
3. **There was no turn.** Nothing reframed the premise halfway, so attention leaked around 15–20 s. Now each story turns at about 55%: the rule has a cost, a stake appears, or we learn who we were wrong about.
4. **The endings were soft and didn't loop.** Now each ends on a twist that pays off a plant from the first 10 s, ideally one that makes the first shot read differently on a rewatch.

## The shape (every episode, 36–44 s)
| beat | time | job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–2.5 s | the strangest, most specific image of the story, already in motion; premise text on top (Inter Display, 2–7 words) |
| **Want** | 2.5–8 s | who this person is and what they want, shown not told; plant the thing the ending pays off |
| **Rule in action** | 8–16 s | the what-if works, and it's fun, funny or useful; small wins |
| **Turn** | 16–24 s | the cost, the stake or the surprise; the premise is reframed |
| **Climax** | 24–34 s | a choice under pressure; the biggest image |
| **Twist / button** | last 4–8 s | pays off the plant; hard cut on the funniest or most unsettling frame |

Text on screen: the premise line (0–2.5 s), at most one label per scene (mono: `DAY 1`, `8:59 AM`), diegetic text (phones, signs, bubbles), and an optional final line. Silent: the story must read with the sound off.

## 01 · What if your shadow got tired of you?  (≈40 s)
**Logline:** A shy man's shadow quits waiting for him and goes after the life he's too scared to live.
**Plant:** every night at the bus stop he sits beside the same woman with a book and never says a word.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** night, a bus stop under a lamp. His shadow on the wall **stands up without him**, stretches and cracks its neck. Text: **"WHAT IF YOUR SHADOW GOT TIRED OF YOU?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** he notices; frozen. The shadow points across the bench at **the woman** (planted, reading), then at him: *go talk to her*. He shakes his head, panicked.
- **8–16 Rule in action:** the shadow does it for him. It slides along the wall to **her shadow** and makes it laugh. Her real self looks up, confused, then sees the shadows flirting. He's mortified and waves "NO" (bubble).
- **16–24 Turn:** the lamp flickers, and **his shadow starts fading** (opacity drops). It's leaving for good. It looks back at him and taps an imaginary watch: now or never.
- **24–34 Climax:** he stands (first time), crosses to her and freezes. Bubble: **"Hi."** She closes her book. Bubble: **"Finally."** (she'd noticed him all along). The bus arrives with a headlight flare.
- **34–40 Twist:** on the bus, through the window, **their shadows are on the wall of the empty stop** holding hands. His shadow turns to camera and gives a thumbs-up. Hard cut.
**Title:** What If Your Shadow Got Tired of You? · **Cover:** a shadow standing up off the wall, the man still seated; text **"IT LEFT."** · **Caption:** "Every night, same bench, same silence. Then his shadow made a move. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 02 · What if every lie added a brick?  (≈40 s)
**Logline:** A people-pleaser walls himself in, one polite lie at a time, until the truth is the only way out.
**Plant:** his friend Sam's terrible new haircut, and the first lie, "I love it!"
- **0–2.5 Hook:** close on a man **walled in up to his nose**, a brick in mid-air about to seal him. He's mid-word: "I'M FI–". Text: **"WHAT IF EVERY LIE ADDED A BRICK?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** label `8:00 AM`. Sam's awful haircut. Bubble "LOVE IT!", and one brick clunks at his feet. He stares at it. Sam doesn't see it.
- **8–16 Rule in action** (escalating, each faster):
  - In bed, on the phone: "ON MY WAY!" Two bricks.
  - Boss: "REPORT'S DONE!" (it isn't). Three bricks crash at once: bigger lie, bigger brick count.
  - To Mom on the phone: "EATING HEALTHY!" with noodles in hand. The wall is chest-high.
- **16–24 Turn:** **others can see it now.** Coworkers stare. He: "IT'S… DECOR." Five bricks slam down. Lying about the lies is the worst lie.
- **24–34 Climax:** night, sealed in the dark. One brick from the top: his phone lights up. Mom: "Are you really ok?" He types "I'm fine…", his thumb hovers, and he deletes it. He types **"No."** **CRACK.** One brick falls out and light spills in.
- **34–40 Twist:** the wall crumbles, and Sam is outside with a terrible haircut. He breathes and says: **"Honestly? It's bad."** Sam laughs and says "I KNOW. I LOVE YOUR SHIRT THOUGH." **One brick drops at Sam's feet.** Hard cut.
**Title:** What If Every Lie Added a Brick? · **Cover:** eyes peeking over a brick wall; **"I'M FINE."** · **Caption:** "He only told little lies. #whatif #animation #shorts #honesty"

## 03 · What if pausing time cost you your time?  (≈42 s)
**Logline:** A man pauses the world to dodge every awkward moment, and spends his whole life doing it.
**Plant:** his best friend frozen mid-sentence: "So, wanna start a ba–"
- **0–2.5 Hook:** a coffee cup **frozen mid-air**, a splash hanging over a laptop. He calmly plucks the cup out of the air. Text: **"WHAT IF YOU COULD PAUSE TIME?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** a café. His friend (cap) leans in excitedly, mouth open: "SO, WANNA START A BA–", and he pauses. The world goes grey. He's not ready for big decisions. A yellow remote in his hand.
- **8–16 Rule in action:** a montage of convenient pauses, label `AGE 25 → 31`:
  - finishing work while the boss is frozen;
  - napping in a meeting;
  - dodging an awkward hug.
  Each pause gives him a little more stubble and greyer hair.
- **16–24 Turn:** a mirror. **Grey hair, wrinkles.** He flips the remote: small print, **"1 MIN PAUSED = 1 DAY OF YOUR LIFE."** Label `AGE 31 → 74`. He drops it.
- **24–34 Climax:** back at the café, everything still frozen since the start. His friend is mid-sentence, still 25. He's old, with a cane, shaking. He presses ▶. Colour floods back.
- **34–42 Twist:** the friend finishes: **"…ND?"** and sees an old man. Pause. The old man grins, takes off his cardigan and pulls drumsticks from his pocket: **"I'M IN."** Hard cut to a garage band: the old man on drums, the friend on guitar, loving it.
**Title:** He Paused Time. He Aged 50 Years. · **Cover:** an old man holding a yellow remote; **"1 MIN = 1 DAY."** · **Caption:** "He paused every awkward moment. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 04 · What if everyone's number showed how much they like you?  (≈40 s)
**Logline:** A man chases the numbers floating over everyone's heads, until the only number that matters is his own.
**Plant:** a mirror in the hallway he always walks past without looking.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** a first date at a café. A number over **her** head: **72%**. He says "SO, I'M INTO CRYPTO–" and it plummets: **31%… 12%**. He freezes. Text: **"WHAT IF YOU COULD SEE HOW MUCH PEOPLE LIKE YOU?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** he wants the numbers up, desperately. He does finger-guns. **9%.**
- **8–16 Rule in action:**
  - **At work:** compliments to the boss take him 41% → 43%.
  - **Gym:** flexing takes a stranger 3% → 1%.
  - **Home:** a dog at **100%**, steady no matter what (planted comfort).
- **16–24 Turn:** he passes the hallway mirror and stops. Over **his own reflection: 4%.** The reflection looks away. Silence.
- **24–34 Climax:** he tries something for himself: dances alone, badly, to the radio, and laughs. The mirror number ticks **4… 9… 22%**. A text: the date, "Fun dancing video?" (she saw his story).
- **34–40 Twist:** second date. He's himself, doing a goofy dance at the table. Her number rockets to **89%**. She leans in, and over **her own reflection in the window: 6%.** He slides her a napkin note: "DANCE?" Hard cut.
**Title:** Everyone Has a Number. His Is 4%. · **Cover:** a mirror reflection with a 4% tag; **"4%."** · **Caption:** "He tracked everyone's number but his own. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 05 · What if every step you took grew a flower?  (≈40 s)
**Logline:** A man sees his whole life as one tight loop, and follows a stranger's trail to find out what he's missing.
**Plant:** every morning at 8:15, a pink trail from a stranger crosses his loop at the same corner.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** from above, a city covered in flower trails. One trail is a **perfect, worn circle**, and a tiny man is still walking it. Text: **"WHAT IF EVERY STEP GREW A FLOWER?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** side view: flowers pop under his feet, home → office → home. Label `DAY 1,825`. Every day, at the same corner, a wandering trail crosses his (planted). He never looks up.
- **8–16 Rule in action:** a time-lapse from above. His circle gets denser; her trail goes everywhere: a beach, a rooftop, a library, a bakery.
- **16–24 Turn:** one morning, **no new flowers on her trail.** The trail just stops. He stops too, for the first time.
- **24–34 Climax:** he steps off his loop. One flower appears in new ground, and the sound drops. He follows her old trail: the beach, the rooftop, the bakery, and smiles each time (living her life backwards). The trail leads through the whole city…
- **34–40 Twist:** …and ends **at his own front door.** For five years she left one flower at his door every morning; he never used the front door. She's there now, holding a pot. From above, his new path and hers form one continuous line. Hard cut.
**Title:** 365 Days. One Loop. · **Cover:** the worn flower circle from above; **"5 YEARS."** · **Caption:** "Same walk. Every day. For five years. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 06 · What if every word cost money?  (≈42 s)
**Logline:** A man goes silent for 60 days to afford the most expensive sentence there is.
**Plant:** the price board, "I LOVE YOU $1,280", and his mom's wallet tag reading $1,280 the first time we see her.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** mid-argument, a man yells "SORRY!" and **a $50 tag flies off his mouth**; his wallet tag drains. Text: **"WHAT IF EVERY WORD COST MONEY?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** the price board: HELLO $1 · THANKS $5 · SORRY $50 · I LOVE YOU $1,280. His wallet: $3. He looks at a photo of him with his mom. Plant: she visits; both silent; her tag says **$1,280**.
- **8–16 Rule in action:** silent life, funny:
  - ordering coffee by charades;
  - flirting by drawing;
  - the wallet climbing $3 → $214 → $890, with labels `DAY 1` → `DAY 40`.
- **16–24 Turn:** the phone: "Mom — Hospital. Come now." He runs. Wallet: **$1,140**. He's **$140 short.**
- **24–34 Climax:** on the way, he sells his watch and his jacket (wallet $1,280, ding!). At the bedside, Mom is weak. He speaks: **"I LOVE YOU."** Tags pop over each word; the wallet drains to **$0**.
- **34–42 Twist:** Mom smiles and whispers **"I love you too"**. Over her head, her tag drains $1,280 → **$0**. She'd been silent for 60 days too. They laugh, broke. Hard cut.
**Title:** "I Love You" Costs $1,280 · **Cover:** the price board with I LOVE YOU $1,280 circled; **"60 DAYS."** · **Caption:** "He saved for 60 days for three words. So did she. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 07 · What if your thoughts were public?  (≈38 s)
**Logline:** At the worst possible moment, a job interview, everyone's thoughts appear over their heads.
**Plant:** the interviewer's coffee mug says "#1 BOSS", and she glances at a sticky note.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** an interview. Over the candidate's head a thought bubble pops: **"I HAVE NO IDEA WHAT I'M DOING."** The interviewer's eyes go up to it. Text: **"WHAT IF YOUR THOUGHTS WERE PUBLIC?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** he needs this job (a thought shows an empty fridge). He tries to think professionally. Bubble: **"PROFESSIONAL. PROFESSIONAL. PIZZA."**
- **8–16 Rule in action:**
  - He tries to hide the bubble with his hands; it gets bigger.
  - Bubble: "DON'T THINK ABOUT THE BUBBLE."
  - Bubble: "SHE CAN SEE THIS."
  - The interviewer's lips twitch.
- **16–24 Turn:** **the interviewer's bubble appears**: "I STARTED HERE MONDAY." Then the CEO walks past. Bubble: "WHERE'S THE BATHROOM?"
- **24–34 Climax:** a wide shot of the office. Everyone's bubbles: "AM I DOING THIS RIGHT?" "EVERYONE IS SMARTER THAN ME." "JUST NOD." Silence. Then he laughs, and she laughs. Both bubbles: **"OH. IT'S NOT JUST ME."**
- **34–38 Twist:** she stamps **HIRED**. Both bubbles flip to the same thought: **"PIZZA?"** Hard cut.
**Title:** What If Your Thoughts Were Public? · **Cover:** the interview with "I HAVE NO IDEA…" bubble; **"NO IDEA."** · **Caption:** "Turns out everyone's thinking it. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 08 · What if gravity took a 10-second break?  (≈38 s)
**Logline:** When gravity switches off for ten seconds, a stranger has one choice: hold on, or save a baby floating into the sky.
**Plant:** the baby's red balloon, tied to the pram.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** his feet leave the pavement, and a coffee floats past his face. A red **10** counting down. Text: **"WHAT IF GRAVITY TOOK A 10-SECOND BREAK?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** chaos. He grabs a lamp post. Cars, dogs and umbrellas drift up. Counter 8… 7.
- **8–16 Turn (early, because the clock IS the story):** a **pram drifts past**, baby inside, red balloon. The mother screams from a bench she's clinging to. Counter 5. He looks at the pole, then the pram.
- **16–24 Climax:** **he lets go.** He pushes off the pole, sails up and grabs the pram at counter 2. They're 20 m up. Counter 1.
- **24–30 Payoff:** **0.** Everything falls. Shake, crash, silence. He lands in the **fountain**, pram held high, baby giggling. The mother hugs him.
- **30–38 Twist:** phones buzz everywhere: **"NEXT GRAVITY BREAK: TOMORROW 9:00 AM. 60 SECONDS."** Cut to the next morning: the whole street is tied to lamp posts. Him, the mother and the pram roped together like family. Clock `8:59:58`… `9:00`. The **baby floats up**, giggling on the end of its rope like a balloon. Hard cut.
**Title:** What If Gravity Took a 10-Second Break? · **Cover:** a man floating while holding a pram; **"NO GRAVITY."** · **Caption:** "Tomorrow it's 60 seconds. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 09 · What if you met yourself at age 8?  (≈40 s)
**Logline:** An 8-year-old version of a tired office worker shows up with a checklist of everything they were supposed to become.
**Plant:** a crayon checklist: ROCKET ☐ · DOG ☐ · FUN ☐.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** he opens the door to **a small him** in a red cap, holding a crayon checklist and glaring. Text: **"WHAT IF YOU MET YOURSELF AT AGE 8?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** the kid marches in and inspects: laptop, takeaway boxes, a sad cactus. Checklist: ROCKET ✗ (red). DOG ✗ (a cactus).
- **8–16 Rule in action:** the adult tries to impress the kid with a "Q3 REPORT APPROVED ✓" email. The kid's face drops; he writes FUN ✗ and holds up a sign: **"ARE WE HAPPY?"** Long silence.
- **16–24 Turn:** the adult opens a closet: a dusty box, and inside, **the half-built cardboard rocket from 1998.** The kid gasps. They build it together, laughing.
- **24–34 Climax:** night. The rocket is finished. The kid climbs in and waves; the kid is fading (time's up). The adult reaches out and the kid is gone. The checklist flutters to the floor.
- **34–40 Twist:** he picks it up. In crayon, newly added: **"FUN ✓"** and at the bottom **"P.S. GET A DOG."** Next morning he walks out with a puppy, and the puppy's tag reads **"ROCKET."** Hard cut.
**Title:** What If You Met Yourself at Age 8? · **Cover:** the kid with a checklist glaring; **"AGE 8."** · **Caption:** "He brought a checklist. #whatif #animation #shorts"

## 10 · What if you got a text from yourself?  (≈36 s)
**Logline:** A man obeys every warning his future self texts him, until he sees who's actually knocking.
**Plant:** "Delete Sam's number", Sam being his only friend.
- **0–2.5 Hook:** phone close-up: a text from **"Me"** · "Tomorrow, 9:14 PM": **"DON'T OPEN THE DOOR."** KNOCK KNOCK. Text: **"WHAT IF YOUR FUTURE SELF TEXTED YOU?"**
- **2.5–8 Want:** the knocking gets louder and the handle rattles. He backs away. It stops. Text: "Good."
- **8–16 Rule in action:** he obeys each text and is rewarded:
  - "Close the window" → a shape passes outside.
  - "Unplug the toaster" → it sparks as he pulls it.
  - He's grateful. The texts are saving him.
- **16–24 Turn:** "Delete Sam's number." He hesitates (Sam is his only friend; a photo of them). Then he deletes it. "Cancel Friday." Done. "Stay inside." The texts are isolating him.
- **24–32 Climax:** a soft knock. Against orders, he looks through the **peephole**: it's **him**, exhausted and soaked, holding a cardboard sign: **"THOSE TEXTS AREN'T FROM ME."**
- **32–36 Twist:** his phone buzzes: **"Don't read the sign."** The lights go out. Hard cut.
**Title:** What If You Got a Text From Yourself? · **Cover:** the phone with "DON'T OPEN THE DOOR." from Me; **"FROM ME?"** · **Caption:** "He did everything the texts said. #whatif #animation #shorts"
